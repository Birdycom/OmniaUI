Planning a kitchen renovation

A new kitchen takes eight to twelve weeks from the first visit to the last cabinet handle. This guide explains every step, so you know what to expect and when we need you.

Before the first visit

Collect a few photos of kitchens you like and measure the room roughly. Exact measurements are not needed yet: our site manager does that during the visit. Read the preparation checklist if you want to be fully ready.

  • The budget range you have in mind, including appliances
  • Which walls, doors and windows can move, and which cannot
    • Load-bearing walls need a structural engineer
    • Moving a window needs a permit in most towns
  • Appliances you want to keep

From design to installation

  1. Site visit and measurements (week 1)
  2. Design and quote, with two revision rounds (weeks 2–3)
  3. Order and production of the cabinets (weeks 4–9)
  4. Installation, worktop and finishing (weeks 10–11)

The planning was clear from day one. We always knew who was coming and when, and the kitchen was ready a week early.
