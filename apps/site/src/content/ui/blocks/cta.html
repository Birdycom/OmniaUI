Call to Action Block

A closing panel with a headline, one line of text and one or two buttons. All blocks.

Demo

Live block with the HTML that builds it (HTML tab). Colors come from the theme tokens, so it follows light and dark mode.

The standard panel: a flat card with the question on the left and the buttons on the right. Make the window narrow (or use the width buttons of the example) and the buttons move under the text.

Styles live in packages/blocks/src/blocks.css. Colors are theme tokens, so everything follows light and dark mode.

ClassDescription
.oc-block-ctaThe panel: a flat card, text on the left and buttons on the right from 44rem, stacked below that
.oc-block-cta--solidBrand-colored panel with turned-around buttons
.oc-block-cta--centerEverything centered
.oc-block-hero-actionsThe buttons: a column next to the text, a row under it on a narrow screen
.oc-block-cta--glowOne soft light in the brand color behind a large centered title; --oc-cta-glow sets its color
