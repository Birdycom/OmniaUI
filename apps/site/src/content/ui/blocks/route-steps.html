Route steps

The ordered list of the steps of a route, a tour or a course in an editor: stops, stations, lessons or tasks. The order numbers are a CSS counter, so they follow the order after every move by themselves. Steps that do not count (intro, photo task, finish) show an icon instead of a number. Install with npx omniaui add route-steps. All blocks.

Demo

blocks/route-steps.html

Selecting and the menus work without script (buttons and popovers); filtering and moving use the optional script.

Route steps v0.16.0

Click a step to select it. Drag a step by the dots, or open its menu (three dots) and pick Move up or Move down: the numbers follow the new order. Duplicate and Delete work from the same menu. Type gate in the search field or click a kind: the numbers of the steps that stay visible do not change. This demo uses the script at the end of the code.
