Shop catalog

A category page for a webshop: filters on the left (category, price range, in stock, rating), sorting, a live product count and cards with photo, rating, price (with the old price on sale) and stock. The filters are a normal form, so they also work on the server; shop.js makes them instant. All blocks.

What is in it
