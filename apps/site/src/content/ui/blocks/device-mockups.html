Device mockups

Show your site where it will be used: on an LED wall or TV with a soft glow behind it, and on a laptop in front. The screens take a real, live page through the free live frame, or a picture. Sharp: only the laptop base is drawn in perspective, never the screen. No JavaScript. All blocks.

Reference

Styles live in packages/pro/src/parts/site-device-mockups.css (OmniaUI Pro). Colors are Omnia palette and theme tokens.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-block-devices / -head / -title / -lead / -stageThe section; the stage puts the wall large and the laptop in front, at the bottom right
.oc-device--laptop.oc-device__lid with the display, then .oc-device__base (keyboard half in perspective)
.oc-device--displayTV or LED wall: the display with a thick bezel and light behind it; .oc-device__stand for a TV
--glowThe light breathes slowly (off with reduced motion); an .oc-accent--* class colors it
.oc-device__displayThe screen: add .oc-live-frame for a live page, or put an <img>, <video> or art in it (16:10)
--oc-device-metal / -edge / -inkColors of the case, its edge and the bezel
