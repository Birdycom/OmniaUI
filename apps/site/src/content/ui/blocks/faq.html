FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start. Can't find your answer? Just ask.

Can I try it for free?
Yes. Every plan starts with a 30-day trial. No credit card needed.
Can I switch plans later?
Any time. Upgrades apply at once; downgrades start at the next billing period.
Where is my data stored?
In data centers inside the European Union, with daily encrypted backups.
Do you offer support?
E-mail support is included in every plan. Pro plans also get phone support.

Still have questions? Contact us

Questions about billing

Short answers to what customers ask most.

Which payment methods do you accept?
Credit card, bank transfer and direct debit.
Do I get an invoice?
Yes, every payment comes with a PDF invoice in your account.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
