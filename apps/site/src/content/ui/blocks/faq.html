FAQ

A questions section: eyebrow, title, lead and the accordion component on native <details name="…">. Items with the same name open one at a time, without JavaScript. Install with npx omniaui add faq. All blocks.

Demo

blocks/faq.html

Questions as an accordion on native <details>: click a question to open it; the one that was open closes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The questions we get most before a project starts.

How long does a project take?
A website takes four to six weeks, a business app three to six months. You get a planning with dates before we start.
Can I switch plans later?
Any time. Upgrades apply at once; downgrades start at the next billing period.
Where is my data stored?
In data centers inside the European Union, with daily encrypted backups.
Do you offer support?
E-mail support is included in every plan. Pro plans also get phone support.

Still have questions? Contact us

Support

Questions about billing

Short answers to what customers ask most.

Which payment methods do you accept?
Credit card, bank transfer and direct debit.
Do I get an invoice?
Yes, every payment comes with a PDF invoice in your account.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.

Billing help center

Reference

Styles live in packages/blocks/src/parts/site-faq.css. Use one name per FAQ list on the page.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-block-faqSection. Modifier --split: head left, questions right (container from 44rem)
.oc-block-faq-inner / -headColumn wrapper / eyebrow, title and lead
.oc-block-faq-title / -leadHeading and intro text
.oc-block-faq-listOn the .oc-accordion: full width
details.oc-accordion-item[name]One question; the same name keeps one open
.oc-block-faq-moreClosing line with a link
