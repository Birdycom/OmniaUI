Content blocks

The editor column of a page, a step or a lesson: every content block is a card you open and close, move up or down, drag by its handle and remove. A closed card shows one line of its content, so a long step stays easy to scan. Install with npx omniaui add content-blocks. All blocks.

Demo

blocks/content-blocks.html

Opening and closing are native <details>; moving, removing and adding use the small optional script of the snippet.

Content blocks v0.16.0

Click a card head to open or close it. Use the arrows on the right to move a block and the bin to remove it; drag a block by the dots on the left. With the keyboard: Tab to the dots, press Enter, move with the arrow keys and press Enter again. Add block opens a menu that adds a new card at the end. This demo needs the script at the end of the code (about 70 lines) because moving and adding change the page.

Fixed places, problems and a drop from the drawer v0.17.0

Some blocks have a fixed place: the arrival text stays first, the way to the next step stays last. pinned shows a pin instead of the grip, leaves out the move buttons, and no move or drop pushes such a block away (Question cannot move up, Image cannot move down). A block your check found a problem in gets issue="error" or "warning": the card edge takes that color, also while closed, and issues puts the number in the head. With acceptDrop (a drag type such as application/x-block) a new block dragged in from the type picker shows the same drop line, and onDropNew(data, index) gets it.
