Form page

Create/edit pages: independent collapsible sections (card-style details) with a two-column field grid, and a sticky save bar that stays in view. All blocks.

Demo

A form in folding sections (General information, Notes) with the actions in a bar at the bottom. Click a section title to fold it. Snippet: packages/blocks/blocks/form-page.html.
