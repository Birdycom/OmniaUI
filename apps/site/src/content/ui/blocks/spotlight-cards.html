Spotlight cards

Product cards with a light that follows the mouse: the glow moves over the card and the border lights up where the pointer is. It builds on the free product card (.oc-product-card), whose glow sits still at the top. On touch screens, without the script and for people who asked for less motion the card behaves exactly like the free one. All blocks.

How it works

  • The script @birdycom/omniaui-pro/spotlight.js listens to the mouse once per [data-oc-spotlight] element (a grid of cards, or one card) and writes the pointer position into --oc-spot-x and --oc-spot-y on the card under it, at most once per frame.
  • The CSS draws the glow there: the same radial-gradient as the free card, at the pointer instead of the top. A second gradient, cut out with mask-composite: exclude, lights up the border at the same spot.
  • The glow still fades in on hover, through --oc-glow of the free card; when the mouse leaves, it fades out where it was.
  • Touch screens (a finger has no hover), pages without the script and prefers-reduced-motion: reduce keep the glow at the top, like the free card.

Reference

Styles live in packages/pro/src/parts/site-spotlight-cards.css (OmniaUI Pro), the pointer tracking in packages/pro/src/spotlight.js. The card itself is the free .oc-product-card. Colors are Omnia theme tokens only.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-block-spotlightSection with a head and the grid
.oc-block-spotlight-head / -title / -leadHeading
[data-oc-spotlight]The element the script listens on: a grid (.oc-card-grid) or a single card
.oc-product-card--spotlightOn a free .oc-product-card (with an .oc-accent--* color): the glow follows the pointer
--oc-spot-sizeSize of the glow (default 22rem, as on the free card)
--oc-spot-ringWidth of the lit border (default 1px)
--oc-spot-x / --oc-spot-yWritten by the script: the pointer position in the card

Use it

// Bundler (Vite, webpack): bind every [data-oc-spotlight] on the page import { initSpotlights } from "@birdycom/omniaui-pro/spotlight.js"; initSpotlights(); // Plain HTML or Blade: npx omniaui copy-js (copies it to public/js/oc-spotlight.js) // <script type="module" src="/js/oc-spotlight.js"></script> (starts on its own; another name: add data-oc-auto) // React / Vue: <BlockSpotlightCards cards={…} /> and <OcBlockSpotlightCards :cards="…" /> bind it themselves

On an existing grid of free product cards you only add two things: data-oc-spotlight on the grid and oc-product-card--spotlight on each card.
