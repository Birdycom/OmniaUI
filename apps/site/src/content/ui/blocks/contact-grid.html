Contact grid

Contact information as a grid of cards: e-mail, phone, office address and opening hours. The icon stands in front of the title, on the same line, in the accent color of the card (no tile). Install with npx omniaui add contact-grid. All blocks.

Demo

blocks/contact-grid.html

The address card is an <address>. Links (mail, phone) are real mailto: and tel: links.
