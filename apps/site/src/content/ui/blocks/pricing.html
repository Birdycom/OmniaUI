Pricing Block

Pricing card grid with plan name, price, features and CTA. Use .oc-block-pricing-card--featured for the recommended plan. Stock and purchasing.

Demo

Three pricing cards (Free, Pro featured, Enterprise). Live block with the HTML that builds it (HTML tab). Colors come from the theme tokens, so it follows light and dark mode.
