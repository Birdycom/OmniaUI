Pricing Block

Pricing card grid with plan name, price, features and CTA. Use .oc-block-pricing-card--featured for the recommended plan. Stock and purchasing.

Demo

Three pricing cards (Free, Pro featured, Enterprise). Live block with the HTML that builds it (HTML tab). Colors come from the theme tokens, so it follows light and dark mode.

Plans and prices

Prices per month, excluding VAT. Change or stop at the end of any month.

Free

For one user, to try it out

€0 /month

  • Quotes and invoices
  • 1 user
  • Support by e-mail

Pro

For a team of up to 10 people

€29 /month

  • Everything in Free
  • 10 users
  • Stock and purchasing
  • Support by phone

Enterprise

For larger companies

Custom

  • Everything in Pro
  • Links to your other software
  • A fixed contact person
  • Agreed response times
