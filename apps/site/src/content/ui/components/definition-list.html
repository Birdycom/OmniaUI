Glossary v0.14.1

.oc-dl--glossary on the dl for a term and its explanation: the term bold, the explanation normal text, and both start on the same line. Term column --oc-dl-term-width (30%).

Definitions

Service
The software and the support that Company 1 delivers under this agreement.
Customer
The person or company that orders the service and pays the invoices.
Personal data
Any information about a person who can be identified, directly or indirectly.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes. Slots: title, description, rows (term + detail), footer.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-dl-panelPanel wrapper (role="region")
.oc-dl-title / -descriptionPanel heading and note
.oc-dlThe dl
.oc-dl-rowdiv grouping one term and detail
.oc-dl-term / .oc-dl-detaildt / dd
.oc-dl-footerOptional footer (actions)
.oc-dl--glossaryOn the dl: term bold in the foreground, explanation normal and muted, first lines on one line. Wrappers: glossary prop.
.oc-dl--flushOn the dl: no margin and no line above it, for a list without a title above it. Inside .oc-prose the rows keep their own spacing and the dd is not indented; glossary terms hyphenate (hyphens: auto, set lang on the page).
