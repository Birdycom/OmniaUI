Flip card

A card with a front and a back that turns over in 3D: flash cards, "reveal the answer", product details. It flips on a checkbox (click or Space), on hover and focus, or with the .is-flipped class. With reduced motion the faces simply cross-fade. No JavaScript. All components.

How it works

  • One cell: both faces sit in grid-area: 1 / 1, so the card is as tall as the taller face. No fixed height needed.
  • 3D: perspective on the card, transform-style: preserve-3d on the inner layer, backface-visibility: hidden on the faces. The back starts at rotateY(180deg).
  • Toggle: .oc-flip-card:has(> .oc-flip-card__toggle:checked) turns the inner layer. The flip labels point at the checkbox and their ::after covers the face, so a click anywhere flips.
  • Reduced motion: under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing rotates; the front fades out and the back fades in.

Accessibility and keyboard

  • The toggle is a real checkbox named by its label ("Show answer"): Tab focuses it, Space flips. The focus ring is drawn on the card.
  • backface-visibility only hides the back visually: screen readers read both faces. That suits flash cards; hide the back with aria-hidden from a script if the answer must stay secret.
  • Hover cards need tabindex="0" and a label, so keyboard users can flip them too.
