Logo

Shows an uploaded logo exactly as it is: its own colors and ratio, never tinted, filtered or masked. A white logo would vanish on a light page and a dark one on a dark page, so only there it gets a calm neutral plate. The choice is made once per image by a small script, or set by hand. All components.

The same four logos in a light and a dark panel. Each logo keeps its own colors and ratio; only where it would disappear does it get a calm neutral plate. Company 3 has enough contrast on both, Company 4 brings its own background. data-plate="auto" lets the small script oc-logo.js look at the pixels of each image once (a canvas of 64px wide) and write light, dark, both or none; the CSS does the rest. This demo needs that script because the colors of an uploaded image cannot be read in CSS. Switch the theme of this page: the plates follow.

light theme
The white logo of Company 2 gets a dark plate; the others need none.
dark theme
The dark text of Company 1 gets a light plate; Company 2 now reads by itself.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/media-logo.css. Plate colors are palette tokens: --oc-logo-plate-light (the dark plate in the light theme), --oc-logo-plate-dark (the light plate in the dark theme) and --oc-logo-plate-both. light-dark() follows the color scheme of the page or of a themed area.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-logo > imgThe logo at --oc-logo-h (2.25rem) high and at most --oc-logo-w (14rem) wide, object-fit: contain, no filter
--boxEvery logo in the same box (--oc-logo-w by --oc-logo-h)
data-plate="light"A white logo: a dark plate in the light theme, nothing in the dark theme
data-plate="dark"A dark logo: a light plate in the dark theme, nothing in the light theme
data-plate="both" / "none"White and dark parts: a mid gray plate in both / no plate
data-plate="auto"Measured once by oc-logo.js (npx omniaui copy-js logo), the React and Vue components or measureLogo(src); the image must be same-origin or send CORS headers, otherwise it stays without a plate

How the plate is chosen

  • The image is drawn once on a canvas 64px wide; transparent pixels are skipped.
  • A pixel with less than 1.35:1 contrast against white disappears in the light theme; one with less than 2.5:1 against a dark page disappears in the dark theme.
  • More than half of the visible pixels white: light. More than a quarter too dark: dark. Both above a fifth: both. A logo that covers 90% of its box with opaque pixels has its own background: none.
  • The result is cached per address, so the same logo on many places is measured once.

Framework wrappers

src, alt, fit (contain or box), plate (auto by default, or set it), height and maxWidth / max-width as CSS lengths. React and Vue measure by themselves; Blade needs oc-logo.js for auto.

<!-- React --> <Logo src={customer.logo} alt={customer.name} /> <!-- Vue --> <OcLogo :src="customer.logo" :alt="customer.name" fit="box" /> <!-- Laravel Blade (+ /js/oc-logo.js) --> <x-oc::logo :src="$customer->logo_url" :alt="$customer->name" />
