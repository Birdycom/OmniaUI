Hero media

A hero over the full width with a video or a photo behind the text. The media covers the section, the text stays in the page container, and a dark scrim keeps it readable. Under reduced motion the video does not play and the poster stays. All components.

Demo

Plain HTML and CSS: the styles come from packages/ui/src/parts/site-hero-media.css, no JavaScript.

Photo behind the text v0.17.0

The photo fills the section with object-fit: cover. .oc-hero-media--scrim-start darkens the side where the text starts and fades out towards the other side, so the picture stays visible there. The section is dark (color-scheme: dark): the title, the lead and the buttons take their dark-mode colors. Below 48rem the text covers the full width and the scrim covers the whole picture. In the demo the height is lowered with --oc-hero-media-min. The scrim stays solid under the text column and fades out after it, also in a narrow box; --oc-scrim-strength and --oc-scrim-size tune it, the same tokens as the scrim component.

Video with a poster, text centered v0.17.0

For a video the components write a muted, looping <video> with playsinline and aria-hidden="true", and put the file in <source media="(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)">. With reduced motion no source matches, the video never loads and the poster stays as a still picture. scrim="full" darkens the whole picture, align="center" centers the text. The preview has no video file, so you see the poster.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/site-hero-media.css. Wrappers: HeroMedia (React), OcHeroMedia (Vue), <x-oc::hero-media> (Blade).

Class / custom propertyDescription
.oc-hero-mediaThe section: full width, dark color scheme, at least --oc-hero-media-min high (default clamp(26rem, 72vh, 46rem))
.oc-hero-media__mediaThe video or img: fills the section, object-fit: cover, position --oc-hero-media-position
.oc-hero-media__contentThe text in the page container (--oc-container); each child at most --oc-hero-media-measure (40rem) wide
.oc-hero-media--scrim-start / --scrim-endDark layer on the side where the text starts / on the other side, fading out; a full layer below 48rem
.oc-hero-media--scrimDark layer over the whole picture (--oc-hero-media-scrim-full)
.oc-hero-media--center / --endText centered / at the end side (start again on a phone)
--oc-hero-media-scrimColor of the dark side of the scrim
--oc-hero-media-bgColor behind the media while it loads

Props

PropDescription
srcVideo or image file
typevideo or image; default video for .mp4, .webm, .ogv and .mov
posterStill picture of the video (while loading and under reduced motion)
mediaType / media-typeMIME type of the video source; default from the extension
altText of an image; empty (default) for a decorative picture
scrimstart (default), end, full or none
alignstart (default), center or end

Accessibility

  • The video is decorative: aria-hidden="true", muted, no controls. Put the message in the text, not in the video.
  • Reduced motion: the source only matches (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference), so the video does not load and the poster stays.
  • Contrast: the scrim keeps light text at WCAG AA on a light picture. With scrim="none" check the contrast yourself.
  • Use one h1 per page: in a page hero the title is the h1.
