Brand mark

Build the logo of a site from an icon and text instead of an image: the mark (an inline SVG or an <img>) with a soft glow in the brand color, the word mark with one part in the brand gradient, and a small tagline under it. Three sizes, for the header, the footer and a hero or map. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-brand-markThe logo: a link or a span with the icon and the text side by side.
.oc-brand-mark__iconThe mark (svg or img), 2.25rem, with a drop shadow in brand 1.
.oc-brand-mark__textWord mark and tagline stacked.
.oc-brand-mark__wordThe name: --oc-font-display, 800, tight letter spacing.
.oc-brand-mark__accentThe part of the name in the gradient brand 1 → brand 2.
.oc-brand-mark__taglineMono, uppercase, wide letter spacing, muted.
.oc-brand-mark--sm / --lgSmaller (footer) or larger (hero, ecosystem map). The sizes are tokens: --oc-brand-mark-icon, --oc-brand-mark-word, --oc-brand-mark-tagline.

Give the link an aria-label with the name, so a screen reader does not read the name and the tagline as one word. In a site header it takes the place of .oc-site-header__brand.
