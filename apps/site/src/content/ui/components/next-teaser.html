Next teaser

A large link card at the end of a product or project page that points to the next one. A small label, the name with its product mark and a tagline with a moving arrow. All components.

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/site-next-teaser.css. Slots: label, mark, name, tagline.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-next-teaserThe card (an a) with hover lift
--prev"Previous" link: the arrow points left and comes before the tagline
.oc-next-teaser__labelSmall uppercase label
.oc-next-teaser__nameName; may start with an .oc-product-mark
.oc-next-teaser__taglineShort line with the moving arrow
.oc-accent--*Accent for glow, arrow and hover border
