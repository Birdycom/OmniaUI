Network graph

A center (a person, an account, a server) with every linked node on a ring around it, colored by level. Nodes never land on the center or on each other: when a ring is full, the next ring starts. Pointing at a node shows its details, a click or Enter reports it, and one node can be lit while the rest dims. No d3 or other dependency; the look is the free graph CSS. All components.

Styles: the free parts/data-graph.css plus Pro parts/app-network-graph.css. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-graph.oc-network-graphThe graph. data-center = the name in the middle, data-center-mark = its sign (◉), data-highlight = the id of a lit node.
.oc-network-graph__list > liOne node: data-id, data-level (low, medium, high, critical), data-level-text, data-meta ("Email · Password"); its text is the name. Without the script the list shows.
.oc-network-graph-legendOne li[data-level] per level.
oc-graph-selectEvent on the graph, detail.id: a node was clicked or chosen with Enter or Space.
renderNetworkGraph(el, data)From @birdycom/omniaui-pro/network-graph.js: draw from data; returns highlight(id) and destroy().
--oc-network-graph-maxLargest height of the graph (32rem).
