Stock

How much is left, in a word and a color: in stock, only a few left, out of stock or on order. The text always says it too, so color is never the only signal. As a line or as a small badge. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-stockA dot and the text. Without data-level the dot is green (in stock).
data-level="low" / "out" / "order"Orange (only a few left), red (out of stock), blue (on order).
.oc-stock__count / __noteA bold number ("12") and a quiet extra ("delivered in 2–3 days").
.oc-stock--badgeA tinted pill for tables and product cards.

Pick the level in your code, e.g. low at or under the minimum stock.
