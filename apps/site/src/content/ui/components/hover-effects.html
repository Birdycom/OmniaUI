Hover effects

One class on any card (.oc-card, feature, link, product, price, project …) chooses its hover effect. All effects share one smooth curve, and a lifted card keeps its hover area, so it does not bounce at the bottom edge. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-hover-liftMoves up (--oc-hover-lift, 6px) with a soft shadow. The product card does this by default.
.oc-hover-growGrows a little in place (--oc-hover-scale, 1.02).
.oc-hover-glowStays in place, with a ring and glow in the accent color (--oc-accent-from, e.g. with .oc-accent--violet).
.oc-hover-noneNo movement; also turns off the default lift of a product or price card.
--oc-hover-duration / --oc-hover-easeSpeed (0.45s) and curve (cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), a soft ease-out) of every effect.

React, Vue and Blade: put the class on the card component (className="oc-hover-grow", class="oc-hover-grow"). With reduce motion on, the cards do not move; the glow still shows.
