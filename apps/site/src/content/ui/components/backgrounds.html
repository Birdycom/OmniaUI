Backgrounds

Decorative layers for a landing page or a dashboard: a grid of thin lines, a soft glowing orb in the accent color and fine scan lines over everything. They ignore the pointer and follow the theme. For drifting points with lines, see the particle field. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-bg-gridThin lines (4% primary) in a 60px grid, behind the content of a positioned parent.
.oc-bg-grid--fixed / --fadeFixed to the viewport (40px); lines that fade out towards the edges.
--oc-bg-grid-size / --oc-bg-grid-lineSize and color of the grid.
.oc-orbA soft round glow in --oc-accent-from; place it with inset-*. Another color: .oc-accent--violet …
.oc-orb--sm / --driftA smaller orb (18rem); a slow drift.
--oc-orb-size / --oc-orb-strength / --oc-orb-blurSize, strength (0.15) and blur (50px) of the orb.
.oc-scanlinesFine horizontal lines over the whole page (fixed, on top, ignores the pointer). --local: inside a positioned parent.

Give the parent position: relative and isolation: isolate so the layers stay behind its content. All layers get aria-hidden="true".
