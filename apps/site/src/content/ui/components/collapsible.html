Collapsible section

A native <details> that looks like a card. Use open for the default state. Unlike the accordion, sections are independent. All components.

Demo

Plain HTML and CSS. Below: the markup that builds it, also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/collapsible.html.

Click the title bar (or Tab to it and press Enter) to fold the section and open it again. It is a native <details>, so it needs no script.

General information

Plain, for a list inside a panel v0.16.0

--plain drops the card (no background, border or shadow): the sections become a calm list with a thin line between them, for use inside a card, a sheet or a block that already has its own surface. The rows are at least 44px high, easy to tap. Open and close them with a click or with Tab and Enter.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes. Slots: icon, title (summary), content (default).
