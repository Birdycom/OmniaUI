Collapsible section

A native <details> that looks like a card. Use open for the default state. Unlike the accordion, sections are independent. All components.

Demo

Plain HTML and CSS. Below: the markup that builds it, also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/collapsible.html.

Click the title bar (or Tab to it and press Enter) to fold the section and open it again. It is a native <details>, so it needs no script.

Plain, for a list inside a panel v0.16.0

--plain drops the card (no background, border or shadow): the sections become a calm list with a thin line between them, for use inside a card, a sheet or a block that already has its own surface. The rows are at least 44px high, easy to tap. Open and close them with a click or with Tab and Enter.

Hint, maybe at a cost v0.17.0

A tip the player opens on purpose. .oc-collapsible--hint draws a dashed frame in the warning tone until it is opened, with a lightbulb, and .oc-collapsible-meta puts the cost (or a count) in the header before the chevron. With once the React, Vue and Blade components keep an opened hint open: a seen hint stays seen. Your app takes the points off in onOpenChange.

Show a hint −5 points
Look up at the clock above the gate: the year is carved in the stone.
Second hint −10 points
It is the same year as the tower of the town hall.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes. Slots: icon, title (summary), content (default).

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-collapsible--hint + .oc-collapsible-metaA tip opened on purpose (dashed warning frame, lightbulb) and a pill in the header (cost, count). Props hint, meta, once (stays open once opened)
details.oc-collapsibleRoot. Modifiers: --dense (compact paddings), --plain (no card: a list of sections inside a panel, a line between neighbours). React / Vue / Blade: dense, plain.
