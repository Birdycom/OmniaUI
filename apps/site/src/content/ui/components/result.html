Result

After a form is sent, the result takes its place: an icon tile, a title, a short text and one or two buttons (Send another message). Give it role="status" and tabindex="-1" and move the focus to it, so a screen reader reads it. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-resultThe result: tile, title, text and actions stacked, aligned at the start.
.oc-result__titleThe title (1.2rem).
.oc-result__textMuted text; links in the primary color, underlined.
.oc-result__actionsRow of buttons under the text.
.oc-result--centerCenters everything (an empty state or a full page).
role="status" tabindex="-1"Announces the result and lets the script move the focus to it.
