Callout

A small card with a short text on the left and one action on the right, centred vertically: a support request, a tip with a button, an update that is ready. When the card is narrower than about 300px the action moves under the text. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-calloutThe card: flex row, wraps, card colors and the padding of a small card (11px 13px).
.oc-callout__textThe text; a <strong> is the title on its own line, the rest is muted.
.oc-callout__actionOne or more buttons; never shrinks. Under the text below about 300px.

React <Callout action={…}>, Vue <OcCallout> with slot action, Blade <x-oc::callout> with <x-slot:action>. Inside .oc-glass it takes the glass card colors.
