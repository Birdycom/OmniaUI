Popover

Free-form content in the top layer, tethered to its trigger. For lists of actions use the dropdown component. All components.

Demo

Plain HTML and CSS. Below: the markup that builds it, also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/popover.html.

Click Dimensions: a small panel with fields opens next to the button. Escape or a click outside closes it.

Dimensions

Set the width and height of the layer.
