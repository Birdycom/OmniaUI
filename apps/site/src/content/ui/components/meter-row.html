Meter row

How much of a limit is used: a label, the amount as text, a bar that turns orange and red near the limit, and when it resets. One row per limit, or a list of them. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-meter-rowGrid: label and value on the first line, the bar and the reset line below.
--oc-meterThe fill, 0–100% (inline style or your code).
data-level="warn" / "critical"Orange / red bar (and a red value at critical). Without it the bar is green.
.oc-meter-row__bar[role=meter]The bar; give it aria-valuenow and a label for screen readers.
.oc-meter-list (--compact)A stack of rows; --compact for a small widget.
.oc-meter-list--denseTighter rows with a 5px bar and a 0.8rem label, for a tray window. No width transition (live values).
.oc-meter-list--liveNo width transition on the bars, for meters that get a new value every few seconds; the bars contain their own layout.
.oc-meter-row__subMuted second line under the bar (tabular numbers).
.oc-meter-row__subbarThin 3px bar after the sub-line text; fill with --oc-meter on it, color with data-level.
.oc-meter-row__fillOptional fill element inside a bar or sub-bar: set its inline inline-size and patch it in place (the bar then draws no fill of its own).

Pick the level in your code, e.g. warn from 75% and critical from 90%.
