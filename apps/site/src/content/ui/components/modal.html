Modal / Dialog

A modal on the native <dialog> element: focus trapping, Esc to close and a ::backdrop come from the browser. Buttons open and close it with invoker commands, so no JavaScript is needed. Header, scrollable body and footer; sizes from sm to full.

Demo

snippets/modal.html

CSS-first: command="show-modal" and command="close" with commandfor open and close the dialog; closedby="any" also closes it when you click outside. The markup is also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/modal.html.

Form with a body

.oc-modal-body scrolls when the content is taller than the screen; header and footer stay in place. .oc-modal-close adds the × button.

Sizes

.oc-modal-content--sm, --lg, --xl and --full change the width; the default sits in between.

Small

For a short confirmation.

Large

For forms with more fields.

Full

Class toggle

For existing scripts: a div.oc-modal with .oc-modal-overlay opens when your code adds .is-open. Focus handling is then up to that script.
