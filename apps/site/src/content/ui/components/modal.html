Modal / Dialog

A modal on the native <dialog> element: focus trapping, Esc to close and a ::backdrop come from the browser. Buttons open and close it with invoker commands, so no JavaScript is needed. Header, scrollable body and footer; sizes from sm to full.

Demo

snippets/modal.html

CSS-first: command="show-modal" and command="close" with commandfor open and close the dialog; closedby="any" also closes it when you click outside. The markup is also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/modal.html.

Click the button: the dialog opens with a backdrop. Escape, the close button or Cancel closes it again, without JavaScript.

Round close button v0.16.1

.oc-close--round makes the close button a circle; it works on the close button of a modal, a sheet and a confirm or alert dialog. In React the components draw that button themselves: closeRound adds the class and closeClassName one of your own (Vue close-round and close-class, Blade the same). A modal and an alert dialog only get a close button when you ask for it: closeButton, or one of the two options. Open it and hover the cross.

Class / attributeDescription
dialog.oc-modalThe modal. closedby="any" closes it on a click outside or Esc.
command="show-modal" / command="close" + commandforOpen and close buttons, no script needed.
.oc-modal-contentThe panel. Sizes: --sm, --lg, --xl, --full.
.oc-modal-header, .oc-modal-title, .oc-modal-descriptionTitle and intro.
.oc-modal-bodyScrollable middle part.
.oc-modal-footerActions, aligned to the end.
.oc-modal-closeThe × button in the corner. .oc-close--round: a circle.
React closeButton, closeLabel, closeRound, closeClassNameThe close button of Modal and AlertDialog (shown with any of the options), round, with an own class; Sheet and ConfirmDialog always have one and take closeRound / closeClassName. Vue: close-button, close-label, close-round, close-class; Blade: close-button, close (label), close-round, close-class.
