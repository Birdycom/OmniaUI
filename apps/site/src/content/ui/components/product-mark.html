Product mark

An app-icon tile for a product logo, glyph or SVG, filled with the accent gradient. Browsers that support corner-shape: squircle draw a real squircle; others show a rounded square. All components.

Demo

Pick an accent with an .oc-accent--* preset. Snippet: packages/ui/snippets/product-mark.html.
