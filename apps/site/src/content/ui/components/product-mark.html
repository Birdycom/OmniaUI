Product mark

An app-icon tile for a product logo, glyph or SVG, filled with the accent gradient. Browsers that support corner-shape: squircle draw a real squircle; others show a rounded square. All components.

Demo

Pick an accent with an .oc-accent--* preset. Snippet: packages/ui/snippets/product-mark.html.

Four sizes of the same tile. The glyph scales with it, and the corners stay a squircle in browsers that support corner-shape.

.oc-product-mark--sm
Small, next to a product name in a list or a menu.
◆
.oc-product-mark
Default size, for a card or a page header.
◉
.oc-product-mark--lg
Large, for a product page or a feature block.
▲
.oc-product-mark--xl
Extra large, the hero mark of a landing page.
★
