Forms & Inputs

Compose forms using .oc-field wrapper with label, input, and optional description/error messages. Use .oc-input-group for prefix/suffix addons.

Field composition

We'll never share your email.
<div class="oc-field"> <label class="oc-field-label">Email</label> <input class="oc-input" type="email" placeholder="name@example.com"> <span class="oc-field-description">We'll never share your email.</span> </div>

Error state (aria-invalid)

This username is already taken.

Input group (addons)

https://
<div class="oc-input-group"> <span class="oc-input-addon">https://</span> <input class="oc-input" placeholder="example.com"> </div>
