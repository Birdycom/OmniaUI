Calendar

Month view markup and styling: a table role="grid" of day buttons with today, selected, outside-month, disabled and range states. The CSS is complete; building the grid for a month and updating aria-selected is your code (or the wrapper component). A native <input type="date"> remains the no-JavaScript option. All components.

Anatomy

  • Header previous and next buttons around a live title (aria-live="polite").
  • Grid a real table: weekday headers with abbr, six rows of seven cells (leading and trailing days in --outside).
  • Day a button; selection is expressed with aria-selected and today with aria-current="date", which also drive the styling.

Accessibility and keyboard

Accessibility

  • Use role="grid" with one tab stop and roving tabindex in your script; arrow keys move by day and week, PageUp/PageDown by month.
  • Day buttons need a full accessible date when it is not obvious (aria-label="Friday, 16 October 2026").
  • Do not rely on color alone: selected days also change weight and shape.

Keyboard

  • ←→ day, ↑↓ week, PageUp/PageDown month, Home/End week start and end, Enter/Space select (script).
