Neon border and button

A border that slowly glows from one accent color to the other, for the one form or card that matters on a page, and a button with a neon gradient. The colors are --oc-accent-from and --oc-accent-to (the brand colors, or an .oc-accent--* preset). All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-glow-borderBorder and glow move from --oc-accent-from to --oc-accent-to and back (3s); works on any element with a border, glass included.
--oc-glow-border-durationLength of one round (3s).
.oc-btn--neonGradient from one accent to the other with a glow, bold with a little letter spacing; muted when disabled.
--oc-btn-neon-inkText color on the gradient (white in light mode, near black in dark mode).

With reduce motion on, the border stays in the first color. React and Vue: variant="neon" on the button; Blade <x-oc::button variant="neon">.
