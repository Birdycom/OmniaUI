Price card

One plan with a monthly / yearly switch, a discount badge and a list of included and excluded features. The switch is a radio group; :has() shows the matching price. No JavaScript. All components.

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/site-price-card.css; the switch is the segmented component. Without :has() the monthly price stays visible. For a full pricing section see the pricing block.
