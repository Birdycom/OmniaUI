Price card

One plan with a monthly / yearly switch, a discount badge and a list of included and excluded features. The switch is a radio group; :has() shows the matching price. No JavaScript. All components.

Click "Yearly". Every card needs its own radio name. Snippet: packages/ui/snippets/price-card.html.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/site-price-card.css; the switch is the segmented component. Without :has() the monthly price stays visible. For a full pricing section see the pricing block.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-price-cardThe card. Modifier --featured: accent border + glow
__head / __name / __descTitle row, plan name, description
.oc-price-card__discountBadge, dimmed while monthly is selected
.oc-price-card__toggleOn an .oc-segmented with radios value="monthly" / value="yearly", optionally a third value="lifetime"
__price--monthly / --yearly / --lifetimePrice rows; the checked radio picks one
__amount / __period / __was / __noteNumber, "/month", old price (s), small note
__features / __feature(--excluded)Feature list with ✓, or ✕ and muted text
.oc-price-card__ctaFull-width button
