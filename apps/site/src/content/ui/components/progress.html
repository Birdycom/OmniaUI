Progress

A bar that shows how far a task is: an upload, a quota or the steps of a form. Give it role="progressbar" with the values, so screen readers read the percentage. For a bar at the top of the page while it loads, see top progress. All components.

Demo

snippets/progress.html v0.4.0

Plain HTML and CSS. The markup is also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/progress.html.

Variants

--success, --warning and --danger on the bar, for quotas and health.

Meter: glow, marker and levels v0.12.3

For usage, quota and budget meters. --glow gives the bar a light top edge and a soft glow in its color, --sm and --xs make it thinner, and .oc-progress-marker is a short upright line at a target or schedule point (style="left: 20%"). Levels: switch the bar to --warning from 70 % and --danger from 90 %; the React, Vue and Blade wrappers do that with levels.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens, so everything follows light and dark mode.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-progressThe track. Add role="progressbar", aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax and an aria-label.
.oc-progress-barThe filled part; set its width to the percentage. Modifiers: --success, --warning, --danger. Token: --oc-progress-color.
--sm / --xsOn .oc-progress: 6 px and 4 px high (default 8 px).
--glowOn .oc-progress: light top edge and a soft glow in the bar color.
.oc-progress-markerUpright line at a target, placed with style="left: …%". --muted for a quieter line. Say the target in the aria-label or in text.

Framework wrappers

The same markup and classes as React, Vue and Blade components.

<!-- React --> <Progress value={60} /> <Progress value={76} size="sm" glow levels marker={60} aria-label="Week usage" /> <!-- Vue --> <OcProgress :value="60" variant="success" label="Upload" /> <OcProgress :value="76" size="sm" glow levels :marker="60" label="Week usage" /> <!-- Laravel Blade --> <x-oc::progress :value="60" variant="success" label="Upload" /> <x-oc::progress :value="76" size="sm" glow levels :marker="60" label="Week usage" />

levels picks the bar color from the value: warning from 70 %, danger from 90 %. Other limits: levels={[50, 80]}. An explicit variant wins. marker is in the same unit as value and max.
