Watermark

A light layer with large text such as Concept, Draft or Sample over an image, a card or a live frame. Clicks go straight through to what is underneath, and the text scales with the box. Plain CSS, no JavaScript. All components.

Draft

Tiled

--tile repeats the text over the whole box, for proofs and previews you hand out. Give it one span per repeat; --light keeps it readable on a dark image.

Corner stamp

--corner (bottom right) or --corner-top (top right): a small label instead of text across the middle.

With a logo or an icon

An <img> logo keeps its own colors at the watermark opacity (--mono makes it gray); an inline <svg> icon takes the watermark color. .oc-watermark__stack puts a mark above a word; logos and icons also tile.

Tones and your own angle

Default, --primary, --destructive and --light; the tokens set color, opacity, angle and size.

Over a browser frame

For a site that is not live yet: the watermark sits on the frame body, under the bar.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/media-watermark.css. Colors are theme tokens, so everything follows light and dark mode.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-watermarkThe layer: last child of a positioned box, text in a <span>; click-through, aria-hidden
--straightHorizontal text instead of slanted
--tileRepeat the text over the whole box (one span per repeat)
--corner / --corner-topA small stamp bottom right / top right
--light / --primary / --destructiveWhite (dark images), brand color, red
> img / > svgA logo (own colors at the watermark opacity) or an icon (takes the watermark color) instead of text
--monoMakes an <img> logo a flat gray stamp
.oc-watermark__stackA logo or icon above a word, in one span
--oc-watermark-color / -opacity / -angle / -sizeTokens for your own look (default: foreground at 12%, -18deg, scales with the box)
--oc-watermark-markWidth of a logo or icon (default 40% of the box)

The text is decorative (aria-hidden). When "draft" matters, say it in the content as well, for example in the title.

Framework wrappers

The same markup and classes as React, Vue and Blade components.
