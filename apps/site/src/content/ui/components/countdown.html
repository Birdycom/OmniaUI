Countdown v0.17.0

The time left for a task, a round of a game or a reservation. Inline next to a title, as a small pill in a header, or in large digits on a screen people look at from a distance, with an optional bar that shrinks. It turns orange when little time is left and red at zero, and it is a role="timer", so a screen reader does not read every second. All components.

Demo

The clocks below really run (the small script oc-countdown.js). The pill starts in its orange "low" state; the bar shrinks every second.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/app-countdown.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-countdown[role=timer]The countdown: .oc-countdown-label and .oc-countdown-time. Give it an aria-label.
--pill / --display / --barA small tinted pill, large digits, a bar that shrinks (--oc-countdown-left, 1 to 0)
[data-state=low|done]Orange when little time is left, red at zero
[data-oc-countdown]Turns on oc-countdown.js: data-oc-countdown-end (ISO or ms) or -seconds, -total (for the bar), -low (seconds; default 20% of the total, at most 60). Event: oc-countdown-end.

Framework wrappers

until (an end time) or seconds (from when it appears), total, low, label, variant (inline, pill, display), bar, paused; React onEnd, Vue @end. Blade renders the time and needs oc-countdown.js to tick. formatCountdown(seconds) gives "1:30" or "1:02:05".
