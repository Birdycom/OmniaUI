Media thumb

A 16:9 video preview tile: the picture, a duration badge in the corner and a thin watch-progress bar on the bottom edge. The bar is a native <progress>. It is the building block of Media card, Media row and Stack card. All components.

Accessibility

  • When the thumb is a link, give it an aria-label with the title and duration ("Morning light, 12 minutes"), or put a visible title next to it.
  • The <progress> keeps its meaning for screen readers; label it ("Watched 40%").
  • Badges are white on a dark label, so they stay readable on any picture.
