Ribbon

A diagonal band across one corner, for a status that must stand out: Paid on an invoice, New or Sale on a product. You choose the corner (top or bottom, start or end), the tone and the size. CSS only; the box it sits in clips it. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-ribbonThe band, a child of the card or image box. That box gets position: relative and clips the band automatically (:has(), zero specificity).
.oc-ribbon--top-start / --bottom-start / --bottom-endThe corner; without a modifier it sits top end (top right in a left-to-right page). Start and end follow the writing direction.
.oc-ribbon--success / --warning / --destructive / --mutedTone; the default is the primary color.
.oc-ribbon--sm / --lgA smaller band (product card) or a larger one (document, hero image).
--oc-ribbon-insetDistance from the corner to the middle of the band (2.25rem); the band length follows it.
--oc-ribbon-bg / --oc-ribbon-fgYour own colors, for example a brand color or a gradient.

Keep the text short (one or two words): the band is about 9rem long and cuts off with … . The status must also be in the content or in an aria-label when the ribbon is only decoration.
