Form density

Keep forms at the standard height; dense editors (the invoice template editor, rows you edit in place) use compact controls. Put .oc-density-compact on the form or any wrapper: inputs, selects, date fields, textareas and buttons inside become 2rem high. No class means the standard 2.5rem. All components.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-density-compactOn a form or wrapper: controls inside are 2rem high, labels and gaps a step smaller.
(no class)Standard density: 2.5rem controls.

The data table controls are always 2rem (toolbar, filters, footer), so a compact form next to a table lines up.
