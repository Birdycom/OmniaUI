Section block

A section card in three parts: a header with title, badges and description, the content and an optional footer. Use it to group a topic on a page, such as a settings group or a documentation chapter. For the header on its own, see Section header.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens, so everything follows light and dark mode.

ClassDescription
.oc-sectionThe card: border, background and radius.
.oc-section-headerTitle and badges on one row, description below; see Section header.
.oc-section-contentThe body, with padding. .oc-section-content__title and __desc for sub-headings.
.oc-section-footerOptional footer with a top border. .oc-section-footer__title and __desc inside.
