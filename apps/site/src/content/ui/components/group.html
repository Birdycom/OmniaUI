Group

One section inside a panel or a widget: a title, a short status on the right in a color (2 busy), a muted line under it and the content. Consecutive groups get a hairline between them. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-groupOne section: padding 14px 0 6px; a hairline above every group that follows another.
.oc-group__headTitle and status on one line.
.oc-group__titleThe title (an h2 or h3); cut with an ellipsis when it is too long.
.oc-group__aside[data-tone]Status on the right; data-tone="busy|ok|warn|error" colors it.
.oc-group__metaMuted line under the title.
--oc-group-lineColor of the hairline (glass hairline or border by default).

React <Group title aside asideTone meta>, Vue <OcGroup>, Blade <x-oc::group title aside tone meta>.
