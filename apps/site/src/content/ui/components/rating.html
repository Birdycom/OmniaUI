Rating

Stars for reviews. .oc-rating shows a score (4.5 fills four and a half stars); .oc-rating-input is five radio buttons drawn as stars, with a hover preview, keyboard arrows and a normal form value. Both are CSS only. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-ratingThe stars of a score. --oc-rating is the score (decimals work), --oc-rating-max the number of stars (default 5). Give it role="img" and an aria-label ("4.5 out of 5").
.oc-rating--sm / --lgSmaller or larger stars (--oc-rating-size sets any size).
.oc-rating-summaryStars, the score (__score) and the number of reviews (__count, a link or text) on one line.
fieldset.oc-rating-inputFive <label>s with a radio inside (value 1–5) and a hidden text ("3 stars"). The checked or hovered star fills the stars before it. --sm / --lg sizes.
--oc-rating-color / --oc-rating-emptyColors of the filled and empty stars (default: the warning token and a light muted tone).

Up to five stars in the input; the display takes any number with --oc-rating-max.
