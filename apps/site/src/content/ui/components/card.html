Card

A surface that groups related content: a header with title and description, the content and a footer with actions. Every part is optional. Use .oc-card--sm for compact cards in lists and dashboards.

Demo

snippets/card.html

Plain HTML and CSS. The markup is also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/card.html.

A card with a header, content and a footer: here a small form. The parts set their own padding, so a card needs no spacing classes.

Accent band v0.16.0

A card that belongs to one mode or tone: .oc-card--accent with data-tone draws a 2px border in that color, and .oc-card-band puts a soft band in the same color at the top, with a mode badge, a title with one line under it, and a status. data-active="false" marks the set that is not in use now: a dashed border and a calm band, still easy to read. The band runs from border to border (an accent card has no padding of its own; the text sits in .oc-card-content) and has a fixed minimum height, --oc-card-band-min-h (4.25rem), so it does not jump when the status comes or goes. Here the row sets it to the height of the tallest band (the status wraps under the title on a narrow card), so both bands line up and both cards are equally high.

Bands in a row, without a fixed height v0.17.0

Put the cards in an .oc-card-grid--bands: the cards share their rows (CSS subgrid), so the bands of the cards side by side are as high as the highest band, also with a longer translation or larger text, and the content below starts on one line. No --oc-card-band-min-h to guess. A card with a footer takes three rows, and the footers line up too.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-card-background, --oc-card-foreground, --oc-card-border), so everything follows light and dark mode.

ClassDescription
.oc-cardThe surface: background, border and radius.
.oc-card--smCompact: less padding in header, content and footer.
.oc-card-headerTitle and description, stacked.
.oc-card-titleThe heading (any heading level that fits the page).
.oc-card-descriptionMuted line under the title.
.oc-card-actionInside the header: placed in the top-right corner.
.oc-card-contentThe body.
.oc-card-footerActions in a row.
.oc-card-footer--divider / --endA line above the footer / the actions on the end side.
.oc-card--fillThe card takes the full height of its grid or flex cell; the content grows and the footer stays at the bottom. React / Vue / Blade: fill.
data-tone="primary | info | success | warning | destructive"A border in the tone and a 3px bar on the start side. Own color: --oc-card-tone.
.oc-card--accent[data-tone]A 2px border in the tone: primary, info, success, warning, destructive, or the modes test / live. data-active="false": dashed, for a set that is not in use now. React / Vue / Blade: accent, inactive.
.oc-card-grid--bandsOn an .oc-card-grid: the cards share their rows (subgrid), so bands, content and footers of cards side by side line up without a fixed height.
.oc-card-bandSoft band at the top of an accent card, from border to border, at least --oc-card-band-min-h (4.25rem) high: a badge, .oc-card-band__title (with a <small> line) and a status. React <CardBand>, Vue <OcCardBand> (slot band of the card), Blade <x-oc::card-band>.
.oc-card-link / .oc-card-textA text link in the primary color / muted body text.
