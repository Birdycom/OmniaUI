Hover card

A rich preview that appears when the pointer rests on a link or the trigger gets keyboard focus. One markup, two engines: browsers with interestfor open the content as a real popover; all others show it with CSS :hover and :focus-within. Anchor positioning keeps it next to the trigger. Anatomy

  • Wrapper .oc-hover-card sets anchor-scope, so many cards on one page do not fight over the anchor.
  • Trigger carries anchor-name; with interestfor the browser handles hover and focus delay and opens the popover.
  • Content is position: fixed with anchor() insets; as a popover it also uses position-try-fallbacks to flip at screen edges (the hover fallback keeps its side). An invisible bridge keeps it open while the pointer travels to it.

Accessibility and keyboard

Accessibility

  • Hover cards are supplementary: the information must also be reachable another way (the trigger link target). Never put essential actions only in the card.
  • The card opens on keyboard focus as well (:focus-within, and the browser's interest handling), but touch devices have no hover: treat it as progressive enhancement.
  • With prefers-reduced-motion the fade is removed.

Keyboard

  • Tab to the trigger shows the card, moving focus away or Esc (popover engine) hides it.
