Document viewer

A PDF, video or image shown inline in a card with a label, and a full-screen view on a native <dialog>. The expand button (or the whole preview) opens it with invoker commands, so no JavaScript is needed. Comes from the service pages of the Verdavita site, where brochures and videos sit next to the text. Install with npx omniaui add doc-viewer. All components.

Click the expand button on the PDF card or the video preview. Esc, the close button or a click on the backdrop closes the view. The previews here are placeholders; put an <iframe> (PDF), <video> or <img> in the frame.

The frame is A4 (1 : 1.414) by default, 16 : 9 with --video and free with --image. In the dialog the same element fills the whole body.
