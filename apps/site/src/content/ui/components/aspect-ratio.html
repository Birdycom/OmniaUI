Aspect ratio

A box that keeps a fixed ratio for images, video and embeds, with presets and a custom property. For a one-off use the aspect-* utilities; this component adds the clipping, background and fill behaviour. All components.

Demo

Plain HTML and CSS. The markup is also available as the snippet packages/ui/snippets/aspect-ratio.html.

A 16 / 9 box at a width of 24rem: the height follows from the width. Drag the preview to the phone width and the box keeps its shape.

16 / 9

Presets and custom ratio

The four presets and a custom ratio, each box at the same width, so you can compare the shapes. Put an img, video or iframe inside and it fills the box.

.oc-aspect
16 / 9, the default: video and wide pictures.
16 / 9
.oc-aspect--wide
21 / 9: a banner or a cinema-style header.
21 / 9
--oc-aspect: 5 / 2
Any other ratio: set the custom property.
5 / 2
.oc-aspect--photo
4 / 3: classic photos and product shots.
4 / 3
.oc-aspect--square
1 / 1: avatars, logos and thumbnails.
1 / 1
.oc-aspect--portrait
3 / 4: book covers and portraits.
3 / 4

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components-extra.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Platform features used

CSS featureRole
aspect-ratioReserves space before the media loads (no layout shift)
object-fitMedia covers the box

Anatomy

  • Box aspect-ratio: var(--oc-aspect) on a block that takes the width of its container (set inline-size or let it fill).
  • Content a direct img, video, iframe, svg or picture fills the box with object-fit: cover.

Accessibility and keyboard

Accessibility

  • The ratio box is presentational; keep alt text on images and title on iframes.

Keyboard

  • Not interactive.
