Tint card

A card that is a link, tinted in one color, with a large outline webicon that sticks out of the bottom corner. The icon drifts slowly (every card in a row on its own beat) and tilts and grows when you point at the card; the arrow chip fills with the color and the arrow flies out and back in. For a row of services, training modules or product areas. Pure CSS; light and dark mode work without extra classes, and "reduce motion" stops every movement. All components.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/site-tint-card.css and site-layout.css. Colors are theme tokens mixed with the card color, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-tint-cardThe card: an <a>, or an <article> with a title link. Tint background (--oc-tint at 9%, 15% on hover), lifts 4px with a shadow in the tint. 9rem high, 15rem from 40rem wide.
.oc-tint-card__headRow with the title and the arrow chip.
.oc-tint-card__titleTitle in the display font; 1.25rem when the card is 16rem or wider (container query).
.oc-tint-card__textShort text, muted, at most 26 characters wide.
.oc-tint-card__metaLine at the bottom in the tint.
.oc-tint-card__goRound arrow chip; fills with the tint on hover and the arrow flies out top right and back in.
.oc-tint-card__artLarge outline webicon (mdi-light:… or a material-symbols:…-outline) half outside the bottom corner, at 32%. Drifts slowly (--oc-tint-drift, 6s); on hover it tilts -8°, grows to 112% and goes to 50%.
.oc-tint-card__linkTitle link in a card that is not a link itself; it covers the whole card.
.oc-tint-card--success, --warning, --destructive, --info, --mutedTones from the theme. Default: primary.
--oc-tint, --oc-tint-foregroundYour own color and the text color of the filled arrow chip.
--oc-tint-mix, --oc-tint-mix-hoverHow much tint is in the background: 9% and 15%.
.oc-tint-card--lgLarger card for a row of two or three.
.oc-tint-card--fill (Pro)The Pro shape: icon inside the card that fills up on hover, a line on top that grows, a 2px lift with a border in the tint. Needs OmniaUI Pro; without it the card keeps the free look.
.oc-section-head--splitSection head with the eyebrow and title (in a <div>) on the left and the lead on the right; one column below 56rem.

React <TintCard href title icon="mdi-light:truck" tone meta size="lg">, Vue <OcTintCard> with slots icon and meta, Blade <x-oc::tint-card> (:arrow="false" hides the chip). The Pro shape: fill on all three. Without href the card is an <article>. Add .oc-reveal--fly to let the cards fly in (Fly-in).
