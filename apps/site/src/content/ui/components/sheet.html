Sheet

A modal panel that slides in from an edge. Opens with invoker commands, closes with Esc, the close button or a click on the backdrop (closedby="any"). Also the mobile container for the sidebar. All components.

Glass, inset and a footer panel v0.16.0

For a menu over a live view (a map, a game, a dashboard). --glass: the panel lets the page show through, blurred, and the scrim behind it is light and not blurred, so the rest of the page stays visible. Without backdrop-filter the panel is nearly opaque, and with "reduce transparency" switched on in the system it is opaque. --inset: on a phone a strip of the page stays free beside the panel, so you still see where you are and can tap there to close. .oc-sheet-footer--panel: the footer holds content (a brand line, a version) on its own background and stays at the bottom while the body scrolls. The sections inside are plain collapsibles. Open it, then try the width buttons of the demo and close it with a click beside the panel.

Floating, rounded and a centered bottom sheet v0.18.0

floating (.oc-sheet--floating): the panel floats free of the edge, with a margin on every side (--oc-sheet-margin, 0.75rem), all corners rounded (--oc-radius-xl) and a border all around. That is not inset, which keeps a strip of the page free on a phone. rounded (.oc-sheet--rounded) only rounds the corners that face the page. A bottom sheet is no longer as wide as a tablet or desktop screen: from 48rem it is centered with --oc-sheet-max (40rem); phones keep the full width.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components.css. Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes. Slots: header (title, description), body (default), footer, trigger button outside the dialog.

Class / attributeDescription
dialog.oc-sheetThe dialog. Side modifiers: --left, --top, --bottom (default right).
.oc-sheet-contentPanel. Modifier: --wide.
.oc-sheet-header / -title / -descriptionHeading area
.oc-sheet-bodyScrolling content
.oc-sheet-footerAction row
.oc-sheet-closeClose button (command="close"): a square with the cross (mdi:close) exactly in the middle. A literal × in the markup is hidden and stays the fallback text; an <svg> or icon inside replaces the drawn cross.
--oc-close-size / --oc-close-iconSize of the close button (2rem) and of the cross (1.125rem). .oc-close--round makes the button a circle.
dialog.oc-sheet--glassSee-through panel (blurred) and a light scrim without blur. Tokens: --oc-sheet-bg (panel), --oc-sheet-blur (16px), --oc-sheet-scrim. Opaque with prefers-reduced-transparency and where backdrop-filter is missing.
dialog.oc-sheet--insetOn a narrow screen a strip of the page stays free beside the panel (--oc-sheet-gap, 3.25rem); a tap there closes the sheet (closedby="any").
dialog.oc-sheet--floating0.18.0: the panel floats free of the edge: margin on every side (--oc-sheet-margin, 0.75rem), all corners rounded (--oc-sheet-radius, default --oc-radius-xl), a border all around. A top or bottom sheet is centered with --oc-sheet-max. Prop floating.
dialog.oc-sheet--rounded0.18.0: the corners that face the page are rounded (--oc-sheet-radius). Prop rounded.
--oc-sheet-max0.18.0: from 48rem wide a bottom sheet is centered with this maximum width (40rem); phones keep the full width.
.oc-sheet-footer--panelThe footer holds a block of content on its own background (--oc-sheet-footer-bg) and keeps clear of a phone's home bar.
--oc-sheet-bg / --oc-sheet-scrimPanel color and scrim of every sheet (defaults: the page background, a dark scrim).
React glass, inset, footerPanelVue glass, inset, footer-panel; Blade the same attributes on <x-oc::sheet>.
React closeRound, closeClassNameThe round close button (.oc-close--round) and an own class on it. Vue / Blade: close-round, close-class. See the modal for the other dialogs.
Overlays inside0.18.0: a combobox list (portal), dropdown, popover and tooltip inside an open sheet stay clickable: the combobox list is a manual popover in the top layer instead of a list in the inert document.body, and the open panel has translate: none so fixed children are placed against the viewport. See combobox in a dialog.
--oc-sheet-durationLength of the slide and the backdrop fade (0.3s). With reduced motion the sheet opens and closes without animation.
