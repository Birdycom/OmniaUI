Fly in

Cards that fly in when they scroll into view: each card starts from the middle of its row, a little lower, smaller and tilted, and lands in its place. fly.js measures the row and marks the cards; without it, or with reduce motion, the cards simply stand in their place. The lift of a product card on hover uses translate, so it keeps working after the fly-in. All components.

Per column, both ways

--oc-fly-step: 45px starts each card 45px per column from the middle, --oc-fly-stagger: 90ms lands the row from left to right, and data-oc-fly-repeat lets the cards fly out again when they leave the view.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-reveal--flyA card that flies in. Hidden only while html.oc-fly-ready is set (by fly.js) and the card is not yet .is-visible.
--oc-fly-xStart offset from the middle of the row; fly.js sets it per card.
.is-visibleAdded by fly.js when the card comes into view; the card flies to its place.
.oc-product-card__badgeA small New label after the name of a product card (with .oc-pill).
initFly(scope)Exported from @birdycom/omniaui/fly.js; returns a function that stops watching.
--oc-fly-stepStart offset per column from the middle of the row (e.g. 45px) instead of the full distance.
--oc-fly-staggerDelay per column (e.g. 90ms): fly.js sets --oc-fly-delay on each card.
data-oc-fly-repeatOn the grid or a card: fly out again when leaving the view, in both scroll directions.

Scroll the page to see the cards fly in again after a reload. The cards of one grid start from the middle of that grid.

Script: npx omniaui copy-js fly puts oc-fly.js in public/js; load it with <script type="module" src="/js/oc-fly.js"></script>.
