Icon tile & pills

.oc-icon-tile (sizes --sm, --lg, --xl, animation --float) holds an icon, emoji, SVG or logo image. .oc-pill is a small label; .oc-pill--accent + .oc-status-dot make a live badge. .oc-status-dot--strong pulses from the full accent with a wider ring; tune it with --oc-status-dot-pulse-alpha (default 0.6) and --oc-status-dot-pulse-size (default 8px). The pulse stops under reduced motion.

Backdrop & reveal on scroll

.oc-backdrop paints slowly drifting color blobs and a fine grid behind the page (.oc-backdrop--local keeps it inside a section). .oc-reveal slides elements in as they enter the viewport using a scroll-driven animation; without support they are simply visible.
