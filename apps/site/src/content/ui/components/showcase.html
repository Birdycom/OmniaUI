Icon tile & pills

.oc-icon-tile (sizes --sm, --lg, --xl, animation --float) holds an icon, emoji, SVG or logo image. .oc-pill is a small label; .oc-pill--accent + .oc-status-dot make a live badge. .oc-status-dot--strong pulses from the full accent with a wider ring; tune it with --oc-status-dot-pulse-alpha (default 0.6) and --oc-status-dot-pulse-size (default 8px). The pulse stops under reduced motion.

Backdrop & reveal on scroll

.oc-backdrop paints slowly drifting color blobs and a fine grid behind the page (.oc-backdrop--local keeps it inside a section). .oc-reveal slides elements in as they enter the viewport using a scroll-driven animation; without support they are simply visible.

Split with a ratio v0.13.0

.oc-split--lead gives the text column more room, --media the image column. .oc-split__media centers an image or a large tile and puts it first on a phone, where an .oc-icon-tile--xl shrinks to 7rem. In an .oc-panel the split works the same.

Hand-written code in a window v0.13.0

Short classes for code you write yourself: .oc-tok-k (keyword), -s (string), -c (constant), -f (function), -m (muted). They take the syntax colors of the window, so they follow light and dark mode.

Backdrop in your own colors v0.13.0

A theme sets the blobs and the grid with tokens: --oc-backdrop-1, -2, -3, --oc-backdrop-3-opacity and --oc-backdrop-grid, on :root or on the backdrop itself.
