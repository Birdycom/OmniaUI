Scroll area

A bounded region that scrolls, with a thin themed scrollbar. .oc-scroll-area is the panel; the older .oc-scrollbar utilities style the scrollbar on any element. Optional edge shadows show where more content is hiding. All components.

Anatomy

  • Region .oc-scroll-area: overflow: auto with scrollbar-width: thin and scrollbar-color from the theme.
  • Shadows --shadows layers local and scroll-attached gradients, so a shadow appears only at an edge with hidden content.
  • Height change the limit with max-block-size or block-size on the element.

Horizontal

--x scrolls sideways and keeps content on one line.

Accessibility and keyboard

Accessibility

  • Scrollable regions must be keyboard reachable: add tabindex="0", role="region" and an accessible name.
  • Browsers that do not support scrollbar-color fall back to their default scrollbar; content stays usable.

Keyboard

  • Tab focuses the region, then ↑ ↓ PageUp PageDown Home End scroll it.

Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/components-extra.css (extensions) and components.css (base). Colors are theme tokens (--oc-*), so light and dark mode work without extra classes.
