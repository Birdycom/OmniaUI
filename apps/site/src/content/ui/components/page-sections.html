Page sections

The small layout pieces every company site repeats, on top of .oc-section-pad and .oc-display: the larger header of an inner page, the head of a section with a see all row under it, a row of buttons, a group title with the number of items, a band over the full width and a meta line (version · date). Reference

Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

Class / attributeDescription
.oc-section-pad--pageHeader of an inner page: padding clamp(4rem, 10vw, 7rem) on top, clamp(2rem, 5vw, 3rem) below.
.oc-display--pageLarger display title for that header: clamp(2.5rem, 6.5vw, 4.75rem).
.oc-section-headHead of a section (eyebrow, title, lead): max 46rem, 3rem below. --center centers it.
.oc-section-moreRow under a section (a see all link): 2.5rem above.
.oc-actionsRow of buttons that wraps: gap .75rem, 2rem above. --center, --end, --tight (1rem above).
.oc-group-titleTitle of a group of cards; .oc-group-title__count is the number in a pill.
.oc-bandBand over the full width with a line above and below (--oc-band-bg, surface); the content stays in its .oc-container.
.oc-metaMeta line under a title: version · date. Subtle text (--oc-subtle-foreground), 0.9rem.
.oc-section-pad--heroHome hero: clamp(3.5rem, 9vw, 7rem) on top, clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5rem) below.
.oc-lead--lg / .oc-display--smA larger, narrower lead (1.2rem, max 36rem) / a smaller display title.
.oc-lead--plainThe lead size (1.15rem) in the text color and the full width, for the intro of a product page.
.oc-text-muted / .oc-text-subtleRunning text in the muted or subtle theme color (a paragraph in a panel, spotlight or split).
--oc-container-gutterRoom on each side of .oc-container on a narrower screen (1.25rem); set it to 2rem from 1400px in your theme for a wide site.

For a row of key figures over the full width see Showcase · key figures (.oc-band + .oc-stat-strip--plain).
