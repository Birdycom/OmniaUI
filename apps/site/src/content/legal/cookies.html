Cookie policy

omniaui.eu sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. That is why you do not see a cookie banner. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

1. What omniaui.eu stores

NameTypePurposeKept
oc-themeLocal storage in your browser (not a cookie)Remembers whether you chose system, light or dark mode. Never sent to our servers.Until you clear your browser data

This is strictly functional: it only does what you asked for (keep your theme). Under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive, in Belgium article 10/2 of the law of 30 July 2018) such storage needs no consent.

Our pages load their fonts and scripts from our own servers. The icon pages on omniaui.eu load icon shapes from the Iconify API, without cookies; see the privacy policy.

2. The checkout (Polar and Stripe)

When you click a "Buy" button you leave our site for the checkout on polar.sh. Polar and its payment provider Stripe use cookies and similar technology there that are needed for the payment and to prevent fraud. They are responsible for those, under Polar's privacy policy and Stripe's cookie policy.

3. Removing what is stored

You can remove the theme setting at any time by clearing the site data for omniaui.eu in your browser settings. The site then simply follows your system theme again.

4. Questions

Mail support@omniaui.eu. If we ever add cookies that need your consent, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.
