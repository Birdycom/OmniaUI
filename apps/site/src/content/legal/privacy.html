Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies. This page explains what we do process. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Serge Cools, trading as Birdycom, Alexander Franckstraat 209/3, 2530 Boechout, Belgium, enterprise number and VAT BE 0633.456.916. Questions about privacy: support@omniaui.eu. This policy covers omniaui.eu and purchases of OmniaUI Pro. omniacss.eu has its own privacy policy.

2. Visiting our websites

  • Server logs. Like every web server, our hosting provider records technical data for each request: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type and the referring page. We need this to deliver the pages and to keep the servers secure (legitimate interest). The logs are kept by our host for a limited time and are not used to profile you.
  • Theme preference. If you pick light or dark mode, your browser remembers that choice locally (local storage). It is never sent to us. See the cookie policy.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics, advertising or social media trackers. Fonts and scripts are served from our own servers.
  • Icon pages. The webicon catalog and a few demos on omniaui.eu load icon shapes from the Iconify API (api.iconify.design and its mirrors). Your browser then contacts those servers, which see your IP address. Only the pages that show these icons do this.

3. When you email us

If you write to support@omniaui.eu, we use your email address, your name and your message to answer you (legitimate interest, or the contract when it concerns a purchase). We keep the conversation as long as needed for that, and for purchases as long as the related records.

4. When you buy OmniaUI Pro

Checkout, payment and invoicing are handled by Polar (Polar Software, Inc.), our reseller and merchant of record, with card payments by Stripe. Polar processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your full card number.

To deliver and support your license we receive and keep: your name, email address, country, billing name and address and VAT number if you entered them, the product and price, the order and subscription status, your license key, and your GitHub username once you connect GitHub. Legal basis: performing the contract, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records. We may keep this in our own administration software.

When you connect GitHub, your GitHub account is invited to our private repository. GitHub processes that under its privacy statement.

5. Who receives data

  • Our hosting provider (Neostrada / Totaalholding, the Netherlands) for the website and email.
  • Polar and Stripe for checkout, payment, invoices and VAT.
  • GitHub for repository access.
  • Our accountant, for the accounting records.

We never sell your data and never share it for advertising.

6. Outside the EU

Polar, Stripe and GitHub are based in the United States. Transfers to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, as described in their policies.

7. How long we keep data

Purchase and invoice data: as long as Belgian accounting law requires. License and GitHub data: as long as your license is active and for as long afterwards as needed to handle questions or refunds. Email: as long as needed to answer you. Server logs: the short period our host keeps them.

8. Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you may object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Mail support@omniaui.eu; we answer within one month. Data we must keep for accounting cannot be deleted before that period ends. You may also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority: gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

9. Changes

We update this policy when what we do changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
