    Mega menu Pro

    A menu item in the site header that opens a wide panel: link columns with an icon, a title and one line of text, and a feature card with a photo. The panel is a native popover, so it closes on Escape and on a click outside without JavaScript, and anchor positioning puts it right under the header. It works in the free site header (also the site header blocks) and in .oc-topbar. All components.

    Demo

    Pro: components/mega-menu.html0 KB JavaScriptBaseline

    HTML and CSS, no script. OmniaUI Pro: the markup is the snippet @birdycom/omniaui-pro/components/mega-menu.html.

    In the site header v0.12.6

    Click Solutions or Resources. On a phone the menu folds into the header's own popover and each panel opens as one column.

    About OmniaUI Pro

    Reference

    Styles: Pro parts/site-mega-menu.css, on top of the free site header or top bar. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

    Class / attributeDescription
    button.oc-mega-trigger[popovertarget]The menu item. Its chevron turns while the panel is open.
    .oc-mega[popover]The panel. Anchor positioning puts it under .oc-site-header or .oc-topbar; without anchor positioning it opens under the top of the window.
    .oc-mega__colsThe link columns, side by side; one column on small screens.
    .oc-mega__title, ul.oc-mega__linksA column heading and its links. A link holds .oc-mega__icon, a b title and a small line of text.
    a.oc-mega__featureThe feature card: an img, then .oc-mega__feature-body with b, small and .oc-mega__feature-link.
