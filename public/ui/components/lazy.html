    Lazy loading

    Long pages stay fast when the browser only fetches what you will see. .oc-lazy keeps the place of an image (no jump in the layout), shows a shimmer or a tiny blurred preview, and fades the photo in once it is loaded (loading="lazy" does the waiting, lazy.js only marks it loaded). .oc-lazy-section lets the browser skip laying out and drawing a section until it comes near the screen. All components.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

    Class / attributeDescription
    figure.oc-lazy > img[loading=lazy]The image box: keeps its aspect ratio (--oc-lazy-ratio, 16 / 10), a shimmer until the photo is there. Give the image width and height.
    --oc-lazy-previewA tiny copy of the photo (a few hundred bytes) shown blurred instead of the shimmer: style="--oc-lazy-preview: url(photo-24px.webp)".
    .is-loaded / .is-errorAdded by lazy.js: the photo fades in / the placeholder stays. Without the script the photo shows when it is there.
    figcaptionOptional caption over the bottom of the image.
    .oc-lazy--thumbA small square thumbnail (2.5rem) for a table row or a list item.
    tr.oc-lazy-row / .oc-lazy-barPlaceholder rows while the next page of a table loads: bars that pulse, --oc-lazy-bar sets their width. Give the rows aria-hidden="true" and say what is loading in a role="status".
    .oc-lazy-sectionA section the browser lays out and draws only when it comes near the screen (content-visibility: auto); --oc-lazy-size is its estimated height (600px).
    watchLazy(img) / initLazy(scope)Exported from @birdycom/omniaui/lazy.js; npx omniaui copy-js lazy gives /js/oc-lazy.js that starts on its own.

    The first demo loads photos as you scroll, the table loads its thumbnails the same way and shows placeholder rows for the next page; the last demo never gets a photo, so you see the shimmer and the blurred preview. Images in the first screen should not be lazy: give those loading="eager" (or leave it out) so they show at once.
