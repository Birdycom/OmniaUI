    Showcase components

    Building blocks for modern landing and company sites: gradient text, icon tiles, product cards with accent glow, code window, browser frame, stat strip, spotlight, steps, marquee, timeline, fact and tag lists, an animated backdrop and reveal-on-scroll. Every color is an Omnia palette token; give any element its own accent with .oc-accent--green, --orange, --violet, --cyan, --red, --yellow or --blue.

    Icon tile & pills

    .oc-icon-tile (sizes --sm, --lg, --xl, animation --float) holds an icon, emoji, SVG or logo image. .oc-pill is a small label; .oc-pill--accent + .oc-status-dot make a live badge. .oc-status-dot--strong pulses from the full accent with a wider ring; tune it with --oc-status-dot-pulse-alpha (default 0.6) and --oc-status-dot-pulse-size (default 8px). The pulse stops under reduced motion.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/showcase.css. Colors are theme tokens, so everything follows light and dark mode.

    ClassDescription
    .oc-accent--{blue,cyan,green,orange,red,violet,yellow}Sets --oc-accent-from / --oc-accent-to (Omnia tokens) for the element and its children
    .oc-eyebrow · .oc-display · .oc-leadLabel, large clamp() title, intro text
    .oc-text-gradient · .oc-text-accentBrand gradient / accent gradient text
    .oc-btn--gradient · .oc-btn--glass · .oc-btn--pillShowcase buttons
    .oc-icon-tile (--sm, --lg, --xl, --float)Accent tile for icons and logos
    .oc-product-card · .oc-card-gridCard with hover lift + glow, responsive grid
    .oc-code-window · .oc-browser-frameEditor and browser frames
    .oc-stat-strip · .oc-spotlightNumber strip, flagship panel
    .oc-steps · .oc-timeline · .oc-marqueeProcess, history, scrolling row
    .oc-fact-list · .oc-tag-list · .oc-check-list · .oc-pillLists and labels
    .oc-site-header · .oc-site-footerSite chrome (see the blocks)
    .oc-backdrop · .oc-reveal · .oc-container · .oc-section-pad · .oc-splitPage background, scroll reveal, layout helpers
    .oc-split--lead · --media · --oc-split-cols · .oc-split__mediaSplit with the text column wider (1.25 : 1) or the image column wider (1 : 1.15), one column up to 960px; your own ratio in --oc-split-cols; the media column comes first on a phone
    .oc-tok-k · -s · -c · -f · -mKeyword, string, constant, function and muted text in a hand-written .oc-code-window (the window syntax colors)
    --oc-spotlight-cols · --oc-spotlight-module-bgColumns of the spotlight and the background of its module items
    --oc-backdrop-1 · -2 · -3 · --oc-backdrop-3-opacity · --oc-backdrop-gridColors of the backdrop blobs and grid lines
    .oc-band · .oc-stat-strip--plainFull-width band (--oc-band-bg) and a strip without its own lines, to put in it
    .oc-split--top · --oc-split-cols-wideColumns aligned at the top; your own ratio that only applies above 960px (the split still stacks on a phone).
    --oc-spotlight-colsRatio of the spotlight above 960px; on a smaller screen it stacks.
