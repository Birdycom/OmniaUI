    Graph

    The look of a small network graph in SVG: a center (a person, an account, a server) with linked nodes around it, colored by level like the severity card, with edges that flow outwards and a pulse in the middle. Draw it by hand, with d3 or with your own code; OmniaUI Pro's network graph lays it out for you. All components.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

    Class / attributeDescription
    .oc-graph / .oc-graph__svgThe frame (accent color --oc-graph-accent) and the SVG, which scales with its width.
    .oc-graph__edgeA dashed line in the accent color that flows outwards.
    .oc-graph__node[data-level]A node group: low, medium, high or critical sets --oc-graph-color.
    .oc-graph__halo / __dot / __mark / __labelSoft ring, dot, sign inside and the name under it.
    .oc-graph__node--center + .oc-graph__pulseThe center in the accent color with a ring that grows and fades.
    .is-focused / .is-activeOn the graph and one node (and its edge): that node lights up, the rest dims.
    .oc-graph__tip (+ -level, -title, -meta)Tooltip for the node under the pointer; data-level colors its first line.

    Give nodes you can click tabindex="0", role="button" and an aria-label. With reduce motion on, the edges and the pulse stand still.
