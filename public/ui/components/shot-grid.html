    Screenshot grid

    Screenshots of an app in a grid: the whole image stays visible (contain, 16:10 on the card color, also for a screen in another ratio) with a caption under it, and a link or a button around the image opens the lightbox. A <picture> with an animated WebP works the same. For the main shot a browser frame with a shadow in the accent color and a product mark on it. All components.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/. Colors are theme tokens, so light and dark mode work without extra classes.

    Class / attributeDescription
    .oc-shot-gridAuto-fit grid; --oc-shot-min is the minimum column width (18rem).
    .oc-shotFigure: image (or picture) with object-fit: contain, --oc-shot-ratio (16 / 10), caption under it; a link or button around the image shows a zoom cursor.
    .oc-browser-frame--glowBrowser frame with a shadow in --oc-accent-from; the screenshot zooms in a little on hover.
    .oc-browser-frame__markA product mark or tile on the bottom end corner of the frame.

    To open the images in the lightbox, put a <button command="show-modal" commandfor="…"> around the image instead of the link (no JavaScript); a plain link opens the image itself.
