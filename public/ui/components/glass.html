    Glass

    See-through surfaces in the "liquid glass" style: a sheet that blurs what is behind it, with a light top edge and a soft shadow, and lighter panels inside it. For a widget or a floating panel over a photo or a gradient, and for a desktop window (Electron, Tauri) with Windows acrylic or macOS vibrancy behind a transparent page. Cards inside a sheet turn into glass panels by themselves. With "reduce transparency" switched on, the surfaces turn solid. All components.

    Liquid glass menu bar v0.12.6

    .oc-glass-bar: a floating pill with a strong blur, extra colour from the backdrop, light inside the edge and a specular rim. The current item sits in a raised bubble (aria-current, aria-pressed or a checked radio). Next to it a round button (--round); --icons for a tool bar. .oc-glass--liquid gives any glass sheet the same look. CSS only; with "reduce transparency" it becomes a solid bar.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/media-glass.css.

    Class / attributeDescription
    .oc-glassThe sheet: see-through, blurred background, light top edge, soft shadow. .oc-card inside becomes a glass panel.
    .oc-glass-panelA lighter layer inside a sheet
    --strongOn the sheet or a panel: more opaque, for text-heavy content
    .oc-glass--flatDesktop window where the OS draws the blur: no own blur, no drop shadow
    .oc-glass-dividerHairline (<hr>) in the glass color
    .oc-glass--liquidLiquid glass: clear tint, strong blur and saturation, light inside the edge, specular rim
    .oc-glass-bar / __itemFloating menu bar of items (icon + label); the current item (aria-current, aria-pressed, checked radio) sits in a raised bubble
    --icons / --round / __sepIcon-only items / a single round button next to the bar / a divider
    --clear / --frostMore see-through / more frosted glass (on .oc-glass, --liquid and .oc-glass-bar)
    .oc-glass-bar--lightWhite icons and labels for a dark or colourful backdrop
    .oc-glass-stageMoving colour background to show glass on (stops with reduced motion)
    --oc-liquid-tint / -edge / -blurTint, edge colour and blur of the liquid glass
    prefers-reduced-transparencySolid surfaces and no blur when the person asks for less transparency
