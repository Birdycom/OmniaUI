    Paper

    An off-white surface with a fine grain, instead of pure white. Use it behind a whole page, behind one section or as a single sheet. There is writing paper with lines, squares, dots or millimeters, and there are paper edges: a tear, the zigzag cut of a receipt, the holes of a ring binder, a folded corner and a stack of sheets. All of it is CSS. Paper keeps its color in dark mode: the sheet stays light, with dark ink. All components.

    Writing paper

    --lined draws a rule under every line of text; the text takes the line height --oc-paper-line (1.75rem), so it sits on the rules. --margin adds the margin line of a notebook. --grid and --dots give squared paper and a dot grid. --mm is millimeter paper: a fine line every millimeter, a stronger one every 5 mm and a thick one every centimeter.

    Paper as the page background

    .oc-paper-theme sets the page color and the card color to off-white, so cards, dialogs and menus inside follow. Put it on <body> for the whole site. With .oc-paper it gets the grain and stays off-white in dark mode; without it the page follows dark mode.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/parts/site-paper.css. The colors come from the Omnia palette. Paper sets color-scheme: light, so it looks the same in light and dark mode and theme colors used on it take their light value.

    Class / attributeDescription
    .oc-paperOff-white background with a fine grain and a matching ink color. On a page, a section or any box.
    .oc-paper--smoothThe same color without the grain.
    .oc-paper--bright / --ivory / --gray / --kraftTones: almost white, yellowish, cool gray, brown wrapping paper.
    --oc-paper-tint / --oc-paper-l / --oc-paper-cYour own tone: a palette color for the hue, the lightness (0 to 1) and the amount of color.
    .oc-paper--sheetA single sheet: padding (--oc-paper-pad, 1.75rem), a 2px corner and a soft shadow.
    .oc-paper-liftWrapper that gives a sheet with a cut edge a shadow that follows the edge.
    .oc-paper--linedRuled writing paper. --oc-paper-line (1.75rem) is the distance between the rules and the line height of the text; --oc-paper-shift moves the rules up or down.
    .oc-paper--marginA red margin line on the start side, at --oc-paper-margin-at (3.25rem); the text starts after it.
    .oc-paper--grid / --dotsSquared paper and a dot grid; --oc-paper-grid (1.25rem) is the size of a square.
    .oc-paper--mmMillimeter paper in real millimeters: lines every 1, 5 and 10 mm. --oc-paper-mm (1mm) is the size of one square (for example 4px for sharp lines on a screen), --oc-paper-mm-color the color (orange).
    --oc-paper-rule / --oc-paper-margin-colorColor of the rules and of the margin line.
    .oc-paper--torn / --torn-top / --torn-bothA torn edge at the bottom, the top or both. --oc-paper-tear (0.7rem) is the depth, --oc-paper-tear-width (15rem) the length before the shape repeats.
    .oc-paper--zigzagThe zigzag cut of a receipt at the bottom; --oc-paper-tooth (0.75rem) is the width of a tooth.
    .oc-paper--holesPunched holes on the start side; --oc-paper-hole (0.36rem) and --oc-paper-hole-gap (3.5rem).
    .oc-paper--foldA folded corner at the top end; --oc-paper-fold (1.75rem).
    .oc-paper--stackTwo more sheets under this one.
    .oc-paper-themeSets --oc-background, --oc-card and --oc-popover to off-white for everything inside. Without .oc-paper it follows dark mode (a warm dark); with .oc-paper it stays off-white.

    Torn, zigzag and holes each use the CSS mask, so combine them with tones and lines but not with each other. A folded corner uses clip-path and ::after.
