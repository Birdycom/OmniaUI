    OmniaUI Pro

    OmniaCSS and every OmniaUI component stay free and open source (MIT). OmniaUI Pro adds the larger app screens on top: dashboards, a CMS admin, a form builder, a media manager, a chat inbox, calendars, status pages and the advanced sidebar and sign-in variants. Every Pro block has a live demo on this site; the HTML, Blade, React and Vue code comes with a license.

    License

    One license per developer (Pro) or up to 5 developers (Pro Team), for an unlimited number of your own and client projects. Using a Pro block in a site or app you deliver is fine; reselling or republishing the blocks themselves, on their own or as part of a UI kit or template, is not. Read the full OmniaUI Pro license.

    • Yearly: updates, new blocks and new major versions while your subscription is active. Cancel anytime; it runs until the end of the paid year.
    • Lifetime: all future updates and versions, without an end date.
    • What you have, you keep: code you got while your license was active stays yours to use, also after a yearly subscription ends.
    • Refund: within 14 days if OmniaUI Pro does not work for you.

    Questions

    What happens when version 2 comes out?
    With an active yearly subscription or a lifetime license you get it at no extra cost. If you cancelled a yearly subscription, you keep the version you have; subscribe again to get the new one.
    How does VAT work?
    Prices exclude VAT. Polar, our merchant of record, adds the VAT of your country at checkout and sends the invoice. Check "I'm purchasing as a business" and enter your VAT ID: EU businesses with a valid VAT ID pay no VAT (reverse charge). You can add a VAT ID later in the customer portal and download a corrected invoice.
    Do I need the free packages too?
    Yes. Pro builds on @birdycom/omniaui and @birdycom/omniaui-blocks (and their React, Vue or Blade versions), which are free and open source.
    Can I try the blocks before buying?
    Every Pro block has a live demo on this site, in light and dark mode. Open any block from the table above.

    Already bought OmniaUI Pro?

    Your license key, GitHub access, invoices and subscription are in the customer portal: sign in with the email address you used at checkout. Questions: support@omniaui.eu.
