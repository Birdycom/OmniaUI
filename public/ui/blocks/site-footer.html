    Five columns with a legal row v0.13.0

    The columns are tokens: --oc-footer-cols (here 1.5fr repeat(4, 1fr)) and --oc-footer-cols-md, the number of columns when the footer is narrower than 66rem (here 4; the about column then spans the row); narrower than 54rem there are two. These are container queries on the footer. A second title in a column (__subtitle), the legal links in the bottom bar (__legal) and the build number (__build); --oc-footer-bg and --oc-footer-bottom-color set the background and the bottom bar.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/ui/src/showcase.css. Colors are theme tokens, so everything follows light and dark mode.

    ClassDescription
    .oc-site-footerFooter with top border and soft gradient
    .oc-site-footer__gridBrand column + link columns (--oc-footer-cols)
    .oc-site-footer__titleColumn heading
    .oc-site-footer__listLink list (also on address)
    .oc-site-footer__bottomCopyright bar
    --oc-footer-cols / --oc-footer-cols-mdColumns of the grid (1.6fr repeat(3, 1fr)) and their number when the footer is narrower than 66rem (3; the about column spans the row)
    --oc-footer-bg / --oc-footer-bottom-colorBackground of the footer and text color of the bottom bar
    .oc-site-footer__subtitleA second title further down in a column
    .oc-site-footer__about pText under the logo (1rem above, max 22rem); a single link under it (a social profile) goes in a <p> too, so it gets the same spacing
    .oc-site-footer__legal / __buildLegal links in the bottom bar and the build number (tabular figures)
