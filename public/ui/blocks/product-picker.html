    Product picker Pro

    Add a product to a quote, order or invoice: a dialog with a search field, the products as options with photo, article number, price and stock (out of stock cannot be picked), a quantity and Add. Enter in the search field picks the only match; a pick fires oc-product-pick with the key and quantity. All blocks.

    Demo

    0 KB JavaScriptBaselinev0.12.6

    Live: open the dialog, search (e.g. "furniture" or "PRD-0004"), pick a product, set the quantity and Add. The data is made up.

      About OmniaUI Pro

      What is in it

      Styles: packages/pro/src/parts/app-products.css (OmniaUI Pro) lays out the page; the parts are free components: data table, gallery, price, stock, spec list, form section, quantity, barcode. Scripts: product-picker.js, form-extras.js.

      PartDescription
      .oc-block-shopThe block (a container): page head and content, full width.
      .oc-block-shop-headBreadcrumb and title (with a status badge) on the left, page actions on the right.
      .oc-block-shop-gridMain column and a side column (20rem) once the block is 60rem wide; the side column stays in view.
      React / Vue / Blade<BlockProductPicker />, <OcBlockProductPicker> and <x-oc::block.product-picker />.
