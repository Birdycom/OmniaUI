    Shop checkout

    A one-page checkout: the steps, contact details, delivery address with the right autocomplete names, delivery and payment as choice cards, and the order summary with totals, terms and Place order. Native validation, no JavaScript. All blocks.

    About OmniaUI Pro

    What is in it

    Styles: packages/pro/src/parts/app-shop-front.css and the head of app-products.css (OmniaUI Pro); the parts are free components (price, stock, rating, gallery, quantity, spec list, tabs, stepper). Script: shop.js updates the totals when another delivery is picked.

    PartDescription
    .oc-block-shopThe block (a container): page head and content, full width.
    Eventsoc-shop-filter { shown } on the catalog, oc-cart-change { lines, subtotal, total } on the cart (from shop.js).
    Blade<x-oc::block.shop-checkout /> renders this markup; fill it from your own data.
