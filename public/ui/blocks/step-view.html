    Step view

    One step of a route, a tour or a course as the visitor sees it: a picture, the title and place, the text, what to do here, the content blocks the writer added (tip, note, link, countdown) and a question. The blocks are free components (collapsible, alert, button, radio cards); the block puts them in one calm reading column. Install with npx omniaui add step-view. All blocks.

    Demo

    Plain HTML and CSS. Your app runs the countdown and sends the answer.

    Step view v0.16.0

    Open the tip, pick an answer (the answers are radio cards, also with the arrow keys) and look at the countdown bar under the time. The column stops at 40rem, so on a wide screen it reads like a phone or tablet screen.

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/blocks/src/parts/app-step-view.css. --oc-step-view-max sets the width of the column (40rem).

    Class / attributeDescription
    article.oc-block-step-viewFrame: one reading column (max 40rem, centered). Container query: answers in two columns from 34rem
    .oc-block-step-view-topBack button and the distance
    figure.oc-block-step-view-pictureAn img or svg. --empty + data-number: a flat surface with the number. -badges: solid badges on top
    .oc-block-step-view-title / -place / -textHeading, place line (icon + text) and the text
    .oc-block-step-view-taskWhat to do here (accent bar on the left)
    .oc-block-step-view-blocksThe content blocks: details.oc-collapsible (tip), .oc-alert (note), .oc-btn (link) and the countdown
    .oc-block-step-view-countdown-countdown-label + -countdown-time; --oc-step-view-left = width of the bar. Modifiers --low, --done
    .oc-block-step-view-question-kicker, -ask, fieldset.-answers (radio cards, equal heights), -send, -hint
