    About OmniaUI Pro

    What is in it

    Styles: packages/pro/src/parts/app-products.css (OmniaUI Pro) lays out the page; the parts are free components: data table, gallery, price, stock, spec list, form section, quantity, barcode. Scripts: barcode.js.

    PartDescription
    .oc-block-shopThe block (a container): page head and content, full width.
    .oc-block-shop-headBreadcrumb and title (with a status badge) on the left, page actions on the right.
    .oc-block-shop-gridMain column and a side column (20rem) once the block is 60rem wide; the side column stays in view.
    React / Vue / Blade<BlockProductDetail />, <OcBlockProductDetail> and <x-oc::block.product-detail />.
