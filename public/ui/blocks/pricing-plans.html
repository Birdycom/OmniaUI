    Pricing plans Pro

    The pricing we use ourselves on OmniaUI Pro, as a block: a free plan first (so nobody wonders whether everything is paid), a launch offer with a code, paid plans with their own yearly / lifetime switch, a featured plan with a "Most popular" tab, a VAT note with a contact line for larger teams, and help when the checkout does not open. The switch is radio inputs and :has(), so no JavaScript. For a simpler table with one monthly / yearly switch, use the free pricing block. All blocks.

    About OmniaUI Pro

    Reference

    Styles live in packages/pro/src/parts/site-pricing-plans.css (OmniaUI Pro); the cards are the free price card. Colors are Omnia theme tokens only.

    Class / attributeDescription
    .oc-block-plansSection (size container)
    .oc-block-plans-head / -eyebrow / -title / -leadHeading
    .oc-block-plans-offerOn a success .oc-alert: the offer above the cards
    .oc-block-plans-gridOne column on narrow screens, then as many cards as fit; the buttons line up at the bottom
    .oc-block-plans-ribbonTab on the top edge of a card, e.g. "Most popular" (use it on the --featured card)
    .oc-price-card__toggleSegmented radios per card: value="monthly" shows __price--monthly, value="yearly" shows __price--yearly (here: Yearly and Lifetime)
    .oc-price-card__discountBadge next to the name, dimmed while the first option is on
    .oc-block-plans-note / -helpVAT note, and an info .oc-alert for checkout trouble

    Platform features used

    FeatureRole
    :has()The yearly / lifetime switch per card, without a script
    Container queriesThe section is a size container for its own layout
    grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, …)One, two or three cards per row
