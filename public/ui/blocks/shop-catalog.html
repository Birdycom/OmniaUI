    Shop catalog

    A category page for a webshop: filters on the left (category, price range, in stock, rating), sorting, a live product count and cards with photo, rating, price (with the old price on sale) and stock. The filters are a normal form, so they also work on the server; shop.js makes them instant. All blocks.

    About OmniaUI Pro

    What is in it

    Styles: packages/pro/src/parts/app-shop-front.css and the head of app-products.css (OmniaUI Pro); the parts are free components (price, stock, rating, gallery, quantity, spec list, tabs, stepper). Scripts: shop.js.

    PartDescription
    .oc-block-shopThe block (a container): page head and content, full width.
    Eventsoc-shop-filter { shown } on the catalog, oc-cart-change { lines, subtotal, total } on the cart (from shop.js).
    Blade<x-oc::block.shop-catalog /> renders this markup; fill it from your own data.
